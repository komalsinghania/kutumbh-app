01 — Primary Mark

The logotype.

The hero lockup. Fraunces Medium for "Roka" — upright, committed. Fraunces Italic for "Maybe" — leaning, uncertain, sindoor-red. The typography is the entire brand idea.

RokaMaybe

Wordmark construction

Single word, camelCase. No space. The capital M at the pivot point is where the tension lives — upright becomes italic, black becomes red.

Font specifics

Fraunces at optical size 144, weight 500 for "Roka". Fraunces italic at weight 400 for "Maybe". Letter-spacing -0.035em. Never alter.

Color rule

"Roka" always in Ink (#1A1410) or Paper (#F5EDE0). "Maybe" always in Sindoor (#C13E2A) or Gold (#B8892B) on dark backgrounds. Never both.

05 — Typography

The type system.

Three typefaces. Fraunces does the heavy lifting — variable weight, variable optical size, distinct personality. Instrument Serif Italic carries the "maybe" — quiet, lyrical, reserved for quotes and section accents. JetBrains Mono for anything procedural — timestamps, UI labels, metadata.

Fraunces
Variable Serif · Google Fonts · SIL OFL
Display & Body — the workhorse
The decision. The discussion.
The year of maybes.

For body copy, use Fraunces at 400 weight, 16-18px, line-height 1.5-1.65. It reads beautifully at every size. The variable optical-size axis auto-adjusts contrast for display use (larger) vs reading use (smaller). Trust the font.

Light 300 · body Regular 400 · body Medium 500 · wordmark, headings Bold 700 · emphasis Black 900 · rare, display only
Instrument Serif
Serif Italic · Google Fonts · SIL OFL
Accent — the voice of the maybe
Before every roka, there's a year of maybes.

Use only in italic. Only for pull quotes, taglines, section accents, or the founder's voice. Never for body copy. Never at a weight above 400. This is RokaMaybe's whisper.

JetBrains Mono
Monospace · Google Fonts · Apache 2.0
Utility — metadata, timestamps, UI labels
STAGE 04 · FAMILY TALKED · APR 22 2026

Always uppercase, always letter-spaced at 0.1-0.2em. Use at 10-14px. Never for sentences — only for tags, labels, status indicators, and timestamps. This is the "operating system" feel.

13 — Voice & Tone

How RokaMaybe talks.

The voice is observed, not performed. Written by someone who's been through the chaos, not by someone selling you the solution. Self-aware. Specific. Allergic to matrimonial-website vocabulary.

We are
  • Witty — but the joke is never at the user's expense
  • Specific — "his mother brought up a horoscope," not "compatibility concern"
  • Honest — we admit this process is hard and sometimes absurd
  • Pro-agency — the user is the decision-maker, not the algorithm
  • Culturally native — we use "rishta," "biodata," "roka" without italics
We are not
  • Saccharine ("find your soulmate")
  • Algorithmic ("our AI predicts 87% compatibility")
  • Preachy ("arranged marriage, redefined")
  • Western-sanitized ("life partner," "significant other")
  • Parent-scolding ("break free from family pressure")
We say
"Stage 4. Family Talked."
Never
"Phase IV: Family Consultation Completed"
We say
"Log the call."
Never
"Document your interaction."
We say
"He asked me to delete my LinkedIn."
Never
"Concern raised regarding digital presence."
We say
"Three calls. Still a maybe."
Never
"You are on the journey to finding your perfect match."
